♻️ Waste Segregation and Recycling Guide

Separating waste at its source helps improve recycling and responsible disposal.

🟢 Wet and Organic Waste

Examples: fruit and vegetable scraps, leftover food and suitable garden waste. Keep it separate for appropriate organic waste processing or composting.

🔵 Dry Recyclable Waste

Examples: clean paper, cardboard, glass containers and accepted plastic packaging. Keep materials clean and dry where possible. Follow your local recycling rules.

🔴 Household Hazardous Waste

Examples: batteries, certain chemicals, paints and fluorescent lamps. Do not mix these with ordinary household waste. Use an appropriate authorized collection service.

🟠 Electronic Waste

Examples: old mobile phones, chargers, computers and electronic accessories. Use an authorized e-waste collection or recycling facility.

🌱 Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

Avoid unnecessary single-use items, reuse suitable products and separate recyclable materials before disposal.

Waste categories and collection-bin colours can vary by municipality. Follow your local authority's rules.
