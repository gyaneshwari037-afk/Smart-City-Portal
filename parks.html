🌿 Tree Plantation & Green Initiatives

Trees can provide shade, support biodiversity, improve local habitats and help urban areas adapt to heat.

🌱 Plant Suitable Native Trees

Choose tree species suited to the local climate and soil. Ask local horticulture experts about suitable species before planting.

💧 Care for Young Trees

Water plants appropriately, protect them from damage and monitor their growth.

🌳 Protect Existing Trees

Avoid damaging roots or bark. Report concerns about damaged public trees to the relevant local authority.

🤝 Community Participation

Join authorised local plantation drives and community clean-up programmes when available.

Record a Tree Plantation Activity

This form records a personal demonstration entry in your browser. It does not register you for an official plantation programme.

♻️ Environmental Awareness

Small everyday actions can help protect natural resources and keep public spaces clean.

💧 Save Water

Turn off taps when not in use, repair leaking taps and use water responsibly.

♻️ Reduce Waste

Avoid unnecessary packaging, reuse suitable items and separate waste according to local collection rules.

🌳 Protect Green Spaces

Do not litter in parks, avoid damaging plants and respect wildlife.

⚡ Save Energy

Switch off unused lights and devices and choose energy-efficient options where possible.

🚲 Choose Greener Travel

When practical and safe, consider walking, cycling or public transport for suitable trips.

🧹 Keep Public Areas Clean

Use designated bins and participate in authorised community clean-up activities.
